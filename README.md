# Santiago Hernández

I lead products from problem framing and design validation through production.

As a Tech Lead, I work across web, mobile, and AWS, use AI coding agents to deliver, and help teams adopt AI through clear rules.

## What I bring to a team

Projects change. These are the skills I bring to each one.

- **AI adoption.** I set up AI practices that are part of the daily work, and that people can follow and review.
- **Architecture and migration.** I design systems that can change without breaking what clients depend on, and I plan migrations so they do not cause downtime.
- **Product design and validation.** Before building, I compare options and their tradeoffs. I make the assumptions explicit and validate decisions with the people involved.
- **Quality and evidence.** I work with TDD and check edge cases.
- **Estimation and delivery.** I split work into clear steps, estimate each one, and mark where a person needs to review. If a question is still open, I write down the assumption.
- **Technical leadership and hiring.** In interviews, I look at how people reason about systems and how they review generated code. I am the tech lead of one team, and I helped development, design, product, and QA start working with AI.

## Public engineering controls

- [claude-code-guardrails](https://github.com/hernandezsanti/claude-code-guardrails): Blocks pushes and merges to main without human approval and limits test workers based on available RAM.
- [claude-code-mods](https://github.com/hernandezsanti/claude-code-mods): Requires a ticket in every commit, protects paid CI minutes, and closes worktrees when a PR opens.
- [ccuse](https://github.com/hernandezsanti/ccuse): Switches Claude Code accounts when one runs out of quota.
- [tupase-mcp](https://github.com/hernandezsanti/tupase-mcp): Provides an MCP server for booking classes.

## A lab for testing ideas with real products

I do the design, the backend, and the app myself, and I use these products to test ideas before I bring them to a team.

- [TorneosPadelUy](https://torneospadel.uy/): Covers amateur padel tournaments across Uruguay, with one Expo and React Native codebase for iPhone and web.
- PadelBump: Brings live professional padel to iPhone through Live Activities, with a backend that checks matches every 10 seconds.
- Pila: Reads Apple Health and recommends how much to train based on your own history, calculated on the phone rather than against averages.
